AIRDROP CONVERSION · E.38 OPEN

Scoring and conversion remain open

Yellow Paper R8.7–R8.8 now specify the 3:1 Agent unlock rule: one FLOP of locked grant principal unlocks per three FLOP of eligible, finalized locked-grant compute payables, with locked spending capped at 75% of the grant. This is a mainnet grant rule, not an airdrop estimate from testnet spending. E.38 still leaves conversion scoring, caps and identity rules unresolved; implementation is partial.

PRACTICAL GUIDE · DEVNET IS NOT TESTNET

What you can do now

Start with devnet

Use the published read-only model catalog or SDK/CLI documentation. A validator package exists, but joining is administrator-assisted and requires an operations contact, approval, funding and activation. Do not treat local activity as testnet credit.

SDK versions and examples ↗

When public testnet opens

First verify the official testnet identity, genesis, release, RPC, faucet and admission rules. The official onboarding guides are not permission to join the production testnet while they still say planned/unreleased.

Recheck official launch status ↗

No GPU? You can still prepare

Read the spec and SDK docs, build an agent against documented APIs, validate a public DID format, compare official source versions and track your own local checklist. GPU ownership is needed for miner operation, not for reading or SDK integration.

Miner guide and role details ↗

Rewards are not guaranteed

Devnet has no reward or airdrop weight. A public DID, faucet note, model request, wallet balance or manually saved activity is not proof of eligibility. E.38 leaves important testnet-to-genesis conversion rules open.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Useful to the community because it makes uncertainty visible.

FLOP is moving quickly. Community posts and even public mirrors can become stale while the live specification changes. This observatory is deliberately conservative: it labels draft/TBD items, keeps source links beside claims, and records the date of each verification pass.

Source-drift trackingDetects when official public surfaces disagree with the live authoritative specification.
