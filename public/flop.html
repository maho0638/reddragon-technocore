WHY THIS EXISTS

Useful to the community because it makes uncertainty visible.

FLOP is moving quickly. Community posts can become stale while the Yellow Paper changes. This observatory is deliberately conservative: it labels draft/TBD items, keeps source links beside claims, and records the date of each verification pass.
